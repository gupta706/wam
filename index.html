01 · Foundations & Literature Review

The Foundation Model Paradigm Shift

Why building thousands of separate ARIMA models is being replaced by unified temporal transformers.

For decades, time-series forecasting meant fitting isolated statistical models (like ARIMA, Holt-Winters, or GARCH) to single series. If a retailer operated 10,000 stores, they maintained 10,000 separate models. When neural networks arrived (LSTMs, GRUs), they improved nonlinear memory, but still required dedicated model training per dataset.

💡

The Core Intuition in 30 Seconds

Just as Large Language Models (LLMs) learn grammar, reasoning, and context across trillions of words, Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) learn universal temporal primitives—such as periodicity, trend momentum, and regime shifts—by pre-training across billions of observations from energy grids, web traffic, and sensors.

Autoregressive Foundation Objective: pθ(xT+1 : T+H | x1:T, c)
Universal Horizon Projection

At test time, the model performs zero-shot inference: you feed it historical numbers it has never seen during training, and it immediately forecasts the future trajectory with calibrated uncertainty intervals.

02 · ICML 2025 Breakthrough

Time Series Foundation Models Can Be Few-Shot Learners

Published at ICML 2025 by Rajat Sen & Yichen Zhou (Google Research) — Teaching models to learn from adjacent series at inference time.

While zero-shot models (like Google's original TimesFM) produced impressive forecasts, practitioners frequently had access to adjacent context. For example, when predicting traffic on Highway 101, traffic patterns from parallel highways or the previous month's holiday weekend offer crucial clues.

Previously, utilizing this data required Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT): taking gradient steps on the model weights for each specific company or dataset. SFT is computationally expensive, slow to deploy, and fragile. Google Research proposed a radically simpler alternative: In-Context Fine-Tuning (TimesFM-ICF).

Why Common Separator Tokens [SEP] Are Vital

If you naïvely glue multiple stores' numbers together into one stream, the attention mechanism confuses where Store 1 ends and the Target store begins. A learnable [SEP] token acts as a semantic boundary, allowing causal self-attention to borrow seasonal cues without confusing raw levels.

04 · Mathematical Machinery

The Foundation Model Architecture Pipeline

Follow a real signal from raw timepoints through normalization, continuous patching, causal multi-head attention, and output quantile projections.

Step 01

Instance Normalization

x′t = (xt − μ) / σ

Removes massive scale differences across series (e.g., megawatts vs microvolts) while preserving intrinsic wave shapes.

Standardization Strength 55%
Step 02

Continuous Patching

pi ∈ ℝP → zi = Wepi + be

Groups contiguous timepoints into tokens of length P. Reduces sequence length by a factor of P (quadratically speeding up attention).

Patch Length (P) 16 points
Step 03

Causal Self-Attention

A = softmax(QKT / √dk)

Allows each temporal patch to attend to preceding historical patterns, discovering cross-scale seasonal harmonics.

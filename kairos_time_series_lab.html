Wold's Decomposition Theorem (1938)

Every covariance-stationary process $X_t$ can be uniquely expressed as $X_t = \sum_{j=0}^\infty \psi_j \epsilon_{t-j} + V_t$, where $\sum \psi_j^2 < \infty$, $\epsilon_t$ is white-noise innovation, and $V_t$ is purely deterministic. This provides the mathematical justification for linear filter representations and autoregressive pretraining.

The PatchTST Complexity Reduction Theorem (ICLR 2023)

By grouping contiguous scalar observations into non-overlapping tokens of length $P$, sequence length scales down from $L$ to $N = L/P$. The attention cost scales from $\mathcal{O}(L^2)$ to $\mathcal{O}((L/P)^2) = \frac{1}{P^2}\mathcal{O}(L^2)$. For standard patch size $P=32$, this provides a 1024× quadratic reduction in attention cost while preserving local phase and curvature!

Takens' Delay Embedding Theorem (1981)

For a deterministic dynamical system on an attractor manifold $\mathcal{M}$ of dimension $d$, the delay coordinate map $\Psi(s) = (y(s), y(\Phi^{-\tau}(s)), \dots, y(\Phi^{-(m-1)\tau}(s)))^T$ is an embedding provided $m > 2d$. Hence, feeding an input lookback window $L \ge 2d + 1$ into a foundation model provides sufficient mathematical rank to reconstruct the true physical phase space attractor!

02 · ICML 2025 Breakthrough

Time Series Foundation Models Can Be Few-Shot Learners

Published at ICML 2025 by Rajat Sen & Yichen Zhou (Google Research) — Teaching models to learn from adjacent series at inference time.

While zero-shot models (like Google's original TimesFM) produced impressive forecasts, practitioners frequently had access to adjacent context. For example, when predicting traffic on Highway 101, traffic patterns from parallel highways or the previous month's holiday weekend offer crucial clues.

Previously, utilizing this data required Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT): taking gradient steps on the model weights for each specific company or dataset. SFT is computationally expensive, slow to deploy, and fragile. Google Research proposed a radically simpler alternative: In-Context Fine-Tuning (TimesFM-ICF).

Why Common Separator Tokens [SEP] Are Vital

If you naïvely glue multiple stores' numbers together into one stream, the attention mechanism confuses where Store 1 ends and the Target store begins. A learnable [SEP] token acts as a semantic boundary, allowing causal self-attention to borrow seasonal cues without confusing raw levels.
